Feature Matrix

Everything Built For India

All 18 core requirements fully implemented with Indian transport realities in mind.

1. Instant Pan-India Rides

Book in seconds with Auto-Rickshaw, Moto, Mini, Sedan, and SUV options with 4-digit security PIN verification.

2. Scheduled Trips (Req 16)

Plan up to 7 days ahead. Free cancellation up to 1 hour before pickup; nominal ₹30 fee within 1 hour.

3. P2P Parcel Delivery (Req 17)

Send small (<=5kg), medium (5-15kg), and large (15-30kg) parcels with pickup photo upload & digital recipient signature.

4. Highway Outstation

Intercity corridor booking (Delhi-Agra, Mumbai-Pune, Bangalore-Mysore, Indore-Ujjain) with toll estimation.

5. Emergency SOS System (Req 11)

1-click SOS button alerts National Emergency (112), Police (100), and broadcasts live WhatsApp GPS tracking.

6. UPI & Digital Payments

Dynamic UPI QR generation for GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, RuPay cards, Cash, and 5% GST tax receipts.
